01
What changed
CHANGE 01
The delivery surface is Salesforce, and Salesforce is a tangled web
Deanna was blunt:
"even for us to make small integration changes, I have to hire a consultant for 100 hours… the infrastructure in Salesforce is a tangled web."
That moves Salesforce integration from a late-stage detail onto the critical path, and it makes Stu Maggs the most important person you have not yet met.
The hardest consequence is not display — it is
feedback capture
. Your learning loop depends on recording what the CA did with each recommendation. In a standalone app that is a button. Inside Salesforce it is custom fields, page layout changes and adoption discipline. Design for it now.
Do
Get Stu Maggs on the next call. Ask what a new tab, a custom object and a dashboard component actually cost in his environment before you promise any of it.
CHANGE 02
A second product appeared: lead-to-advisor matching
John led with it and it is not in any scope you have written. He wants the agent to route each lead to the person most likely to convert it:
"Gus is really good dealing with this type of person… Claire is the best at closing this type of executive."
Deanna offered to build the input — a database of the BD and CA teams with certifications, strengths and geography.
This is a genuinely different model from prospect ranking. It needs per-person historical conversion broken down by segment, and TMG almost certainly has not assembled that. Tom's current routing is geography plus a lead-owner field.
Do
Scope it separately and say so. It is a strong second phase, and it will stall Phase 1 if it gets bundled in.
CHANGE 03
The bottleneck has a name, and it may not be yours to fix
Tom named it twice, unprompted:
there is no suppression between WealthFeed and Salesforce.
Every CA manually cross-references each prospect to check they are not already in the database. Marketing hits the same wall on ABM campaigns. Deanna hits it again in VisitIQ —
"clients are being suppressed out of here, we're still figuring that out."
This is your Phase 0, now validated by the user rather than hypothesised. But note the awkward part:
suppression is arguably WealthFeed's job
, and Deanna has the CEO's mobile. Raise it with them first. Winning by making the client's existing vendor fix their own gap buys more trust than building around it.
Do
Put suppression at the top of the WealthFeed vendor call. If they will not ship it, you build it and it is a fast, provable win.
CHANGE 04
WealthFeed is narrower than feared — the overlap risk is largely gone
The question you most needed answered got answered. WealthFeed is used for
identification only
: search by ICP criteria, wealth data, and "catalysts" (inheritance, job change, relocation). Rolled out firm-wide at the start of July after an eight-week pilot. Tom reports immediate impact on client size and pipeline.
No outreach automation, no sequencer, no Proofpoint compliance flow in use.
CAs call and email by hand. That leaves the entire decision layer, the messaging layer and the supervision layer open — which is the scope you wanted.
Do
Stop treating WealthFeed as a competitor. It is an upstream data source. Say that plainly next time; it will land well.
CHANGE 05
Tom handed you the narrowest valuable first feature
"If I make a phone call and an AI agent, compliance approved, sends an email following my phone call, that's something that would definitely create some more efficiency."
That is small, unambiguous, sits exactly on your existing compliance capability, and the end user asked for it himself. It also fits the day-1 cadence he described — call, voicemail, email, all on the same day — and the copy-paste burden he called a bottleneck.
Do
Consider making this the visible Phase 1 alongside suppression. Adoption is guaranteed when the user specified the feature.
CHANGE 06
The best dataset in the building is untouched
Every prospect and client meeting is recorded in
Zoom Revenue Accelerator
. Gevorg spotted it and nobody followed up. With a 34% close rate over four meetings and cycles from three weeks to years, those recordings hold the actual reasons deals convert — the thing no CRM field captures.
Do
Add it to the data request. Ask what Revenue Accelerator exposes via API, and whether transcripts can be exported.
